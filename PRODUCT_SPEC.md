# Product specification

## Purpose

Presnya helps a person keep a clear, humane view of what matters today without turning life into a dashboard.

## Initial outcome

The first usable slice should let a person open the app, see a calm daily view, and understand the next useful action immediately.

## Scope of the first slice

- Daily view with a small number of priorities.
- Local data model first; sync is deliberately deferred.
- Health endpoint for the backend foundation.
- SwiftUI shell that can evolve without committing to final visual design.

## Non-goals

- Social features.
- Notifications and gamification.
- Complex analytics.
- Premature integrations.

## Success signal

The user can return to the app after a pause and recover context in under ten seconds.
